Frequently Asked Questions

For any unanswered questions, reach out to our support team via email. We'll respond as soon as possible to assist you.

What is Castora?

Castora is a social media scheduling platform with AI built in. Plan posts on a calendar, write them with AI and publish to all your channels from one place.

Which platforms can I publish to?

Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, Reddit, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon, Telegram, Discord and more.

Can my team work together in Castora?

Yes. Invite teammates, assign roles, comment on drafts and share preview links so clients can approve posts.

How does the AI work?

Castora's AI assistant and agents research topics, write and repurpose posts, and can schedule them for you.

Can I try Castora for free?

Yes. Sign up and start scheduling for free.
